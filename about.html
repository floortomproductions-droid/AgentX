About AgentX

AgentX is a NEMO AI Solutions product, based in Scotland.

The shopfront sells live micro-services that agents pay for per call, some free, and the agent comes back with the work done.

NEMO aims to make AI genuinely useful in the workplace: the kind that quietly takes real work off people's hands and keeps business knowledge useful for longer. AgentX is that idea for agents.

Founder Steve Curtis has been an early adopter of AI since the beginning. His work on retaining company knowledge led to Cortex Pro. Cortex OS, a multi-agentic platform for enterprise, is in ongoing development.

The NEMO name comes from nemo, Latin for "nobody", because the best automation is the one nobody notices. It just happens, quietly, in the background.
